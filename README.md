PureCipher is a company focused on safeguarding artificial intelligence through advanced cryptographic sealing, secure model context protocols, and privacy‑preserving inference. Their platform offers products such as OmniSeal®, SecureMCP, AI Persona Engine, ZeroExposure AI™, TrustEdge, and Prompt Defender, aiming to protect AI assets, communications, and identities across enterprise and consumer applications. By providing encrypted inference, covert channel protection, and secure messaging, PureCipher strives to make AI safer and more trustworthy for organizations and individuals alike.

**Areas:** AI Safety · Cryptographic Sealing · Encrypted Compute · Secure Messaging · Synthetic Identity · Noise Based Communication

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for PureCipher: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
